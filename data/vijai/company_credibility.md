# VijAI Credibility Information

The VijAI website currently presents the following company-level figures:

- 10 Million+ data points processed
- 30,000+ total training hours
- 18,200+ unsafe acts detected
- 95.8% prediction accuracy
- 100+ deployed sites
- 42% incident reduction

The website also states that VijAI serves multiple industries,
including retail.

## Important Sales Rule

These numbers are company-level claims.

Do NOT automatically represent them as FIREAI-specific metrics.

For example:

Incorrect:

"FIREAI has 95.8% accuracy."

Better:

"VijAI reports 95.8% prediction accuracy at the company/platform
level. FIREAI-specific performance should be validated under the
customer's deployment conditions."

Do not invent named FIREAI customers unless a customer is explicitly
included in the grounding documents.
