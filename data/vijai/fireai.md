# VijAI FIREAI

## Product

FIREAI is an AI-powered fire and smoke detection system.

It continuously monitors CCTV feeds and identifies signs of fire
or smoke.

The system is designed to provide real-time detection and trigger
alerts/alarm mechanisms for rapid response.

## Deployment

FIREAI supports:

- On-premise deployment
- Cloud deployment

## Relevant Environments

The VijAI website describes FIREAI applications in:

- Warehouses
- EHS environments
- Maintenance areas
- Logistics
- Substations
- Operations

## Warehouse

FIREAI can monitor storage environments for early indications
of fire or smoke.

## Maintenance

The product description mentions detecting situations such as:

- Electrical short circuits
- Overheating machinery
- Sparks

before they become larger fire hazards.

## Logistics

FIREAI can monitor:

- Loading bays
- Parking areas
- Transport hubs

## Operations

FIREAI provides continuous AI-based monitoring of production
and process areas.
