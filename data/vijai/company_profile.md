# VijAI Company Profile

VijAI is an AI-based vigilance solution offered by VB Group.

VijAI provides AI-powered video analytics and safety solutions.

The platform includes modules such as:

- PPEAI
- FIREAI
- GESTEAI
- DRIVEAI
- ZONEAI
- PULSEAI
- SAFEAI
- SECUREAI
- GUARDAI

VijAI states that its solutions can operate in both:

- On-premise environments
- Cloud environments
