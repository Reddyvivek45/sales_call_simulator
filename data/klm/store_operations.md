# KLM Store Operations

## Retail Environment

KLM operates fashion retail environments with multiple departments and,
according to public customer feedback on its website, some stores have
multiple floors.

Typical areas relevant to fire safety include:

- Sales floors
- Clothing display areas
- Storage areas
- Billing counters
- Electrical rooms
- Utility areas
- Parking areas
- Loading/unloading areas
- Back-office areas
- Staff areas

## FireAI-Relevant Risk Areas

For simulation purposes, the buyer is particularly concerned about:

1. Electrical faults
2. Smoke developing outside direct customer visibility
3. Fire in storage areas
4. Fire during high-footfall periods
5. Fire during festive events
6. False alarms causing unnecessary evacuation
7. Blind spots in CCTV coverage
8. Response time after an alarm
9. Monitoring multiple branches
10. Integration with existing security operations

## Existing Infrastructure

The exact CCTV architecture, camera count, NVR/VMS,
network topology and fire alarm system are NOT known.

The salesperson should ask discovery questions before proposing
a final deployment architecture.

## Buyer Position

The buyer does not want to replace existing CCTV infrastructure
unless there is a strong technical or financial reason.

The buyer prefers solutions that can use existing infrastructure
where technically feasible.
