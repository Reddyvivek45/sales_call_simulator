# KLM Fashion Mall — Company Profile

## Company

KLM Fashion Mall is a fashion retail business operating shopping/fashion mall
stores in India.

The public website references operations in Hyderabad and Bengaluru.

## Retail Categories

KLM's website shows a broad fashion retail assortment including:

- Men's western wear
- Men's formal wear
- Men's casual wear
- Men's ethnic wear
- Women's formal wear
- Women's western wear
- Women's ethnic wear
- Women's sarees
- Women's fusion wear
- Girls' festive wear
- Girls' western wear
- Girls' casual wear
- Boys' casual wear
- Boys' ethnic wear

## Customer Environment

The business serves family-oriented retail customers and operates large
fashion-store environments.

The website includes customer feedback mentioning:

- Multiple floors
- Separate sections for men, women and children
- Parking
- Valet parking
- High customer traffic
- Seasonal and festive shopping
- Multiple branches in Hyderabad and Bengaluru
