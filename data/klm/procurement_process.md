# KLM Procurement Context

## Procurement Considerations

A potential FIREAI purchase may require evaluation of:

- Business requirement
- Technical feasibility
- Commercial proposal
- Pilot or proof of concept
- Expected ROI
- Deployment requirements
- Maintenance responsibilities
- Scalability

## Decision Process

The exact KLM decision-making hierarchy is currently unknown.

Do not invent:

- Decision-maker names
- Approval limits
- Procurement policies
- Budget thresholds
- Contract terms
- Purchase timelines

If asked about these details, state that they need to be verified.

## Pilot Evaluation

A pilot may be used to evaluate:

- Detection performance
- False alarms
- Operational usability
- CCTV compatibility
- Alert response
- Deployment requirements

Exact pilot duration and KPIs are not currently defined.
