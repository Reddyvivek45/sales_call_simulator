# KLM Business Risks

KLM may consider the following risks when evaluating FIREAI:

## False Alarms

Excessive false alarms could:

- Cause unnecessary disruption
- Reduce trust in the system
- Create unnecessary evacuations
- Increase operational workload

## Missed Events

Failure to detect a relevant event could reduce
confidence in the solution.

## Deployment Risk

A deployment that requires major changes to existing
infrastructure may increase cost and complexity.

## Operational Risk

The solution should fit into existing security and
fire-safety operations.

## Commercial Risk

KLM needs to understand the total cost and whether
the expected business value justifies the investment.

## Scaling Risk

A solution that works for one location may need to be
evaluated before expanding across multiple branches.
