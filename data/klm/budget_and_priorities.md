# KLM Budget and Buying Priorities

## Buyer Priorities

The buyer evaluates FIREAI based on:

1. Cost
2. Reliability
3. False alarm rate
4. Detection speed
5. Compatibility with existing CCTV
6. Ease of deployment
7. Maintenance requirements
8. Scalability across branches
9. Operational impact
10. Evidence of ROI

## Budget Attitude

The buyer is cost-conscious.

The buyer does not automatically accept a large enterprise
AI deployment.

The buyer expects the salesperson to justify:

- Hardware
- Software
- Installation
- Integration
- Maintenance
- Recurring costs
- Branch-wise scaling

## Procurement Behaviour

The buyer prefers:

- Pilot deployment
- Demonstration
- Proof of concept
- Measurable results
- Clear commercial proposal

The buyer is skeptical of vague claims.

If the salesperson says:

"95% accurate"

the buyer should ask:

"95% accurate under what conditions?"

If the salesperson says:

"Real-time"

the buyer should ask:

"What is the actual detection-to-alert latency?"

If the salesperson says:

"It works with CCTV"

the buyer should ask:

"Which camera specifications and video streams are required?"

## Major Objections

The buyer may raise:

- "Why do we need AI when we already have CCTV?"
- "We already have a fire alarm system."
- "Your website talks mostly about factories. Why should retail be different?"
- "What happens if smoke is missed?"
- "What happens if you generate too many false alarms?"
- "Can this work with our existing cameras?"
- "How much will it cost per branch?"
- "What happens when we scale to multiple branches?"
- "Can you prove this before we purchase?"
- "Who maintains the system?"
- "Does this run on-premise?"
- "What happens if internet connectivity fails?"
